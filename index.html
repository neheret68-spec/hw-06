About the school

About us

English Excellence is an online English school that helps students learn the language through practical lessons, modern materials and individual support from teachers. We combine speaking practice, grammar explanations, vocabulary training and feedback after each lesson. Students can study at a comfortable pace, repeat difficult topics and choose a course format according to their goals.
